Our Calling & Testimony

A Beacon of Christ's Love in Palliwada

CSI Wesley Church, Palliwada warmly welcomes all youth, students, and believers in the precious name of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ. Under the Church of South India (CSI), Diocese of Karimnagar, Aler Group Church Council, and Mothkur Pastorate, our church stands in Palliwada village in Yadadri Bhuvanagiri district of Telangana as a sanctuary of prayer, spiritual renewal, and active fellowship.

Grounded in Wesleyan evangelical heritage and biblical truth, our congregation is committed to preaching the Word of God, encouraging family values, fostering prayer, and equipping the next generation of youth to stand firm in their faith in Christ Jesus under the divine motto CALLED. CHOSEN. LOVED. SENT.

"Our prayer is that every young boy and girl who enters this church discovers the saving grace of God, discovers their divine purpose, and abides fruitfully in Christ."
