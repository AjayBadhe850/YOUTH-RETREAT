About Wesley Church Palliwada

A Sanctuary of Faith, Fellowship, and Discipleship in Telangana

Our Calling & Testimony

A Beacon of Christ's Love in Palliwada

Wesley Church Palliwada warmly welcomes all youth, students, and believers in the precious name of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ. Nestled in Palliwada village in the Yadadri Bhuvanagiri district of Telangana, our church stands as a sanctuary of prayer, spiritual renewal, and active community fellowship for young people.

Grounded in Wesleyan evangelical heritage and biblical truth, our congregation is committed to preaching the Word of God, encouraging family values, fostering prayer, and equipping the next generation of youth to stand firm in their faith in Christ Jesus.

"Our prayer is that every young boy and girl who enters this church discovers the saving grace of God, discovers their divine purpose, and becomes an ambassador of peace and light."
