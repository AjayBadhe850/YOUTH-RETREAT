One-Day Youth Retreat

Arise and Shine — 1 Timothy 4:12

Theme Meditation

Arise and Shine — 1 Timothy 4:12

"Let no one despise your youth, but be an example to the believers in word, in conduct, in love, in spirit, in faith, in purity." — 1 Timothy 4:12

The One-Day Youth Retreat is a special spiritual gathering bringing together youth from across congregations for a day dedicated to God. Set apart from daily routines, this retreat offers heartfelt praise and adoration, empowering biblical preaching, group fellowship, and prayer for our personal walks, families, and education.

Key Event Facts
Date: To Be Announced
Timings: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM IST
Venue: Wesley Church Sanctuary, Palliwada
Admission: Free Admission
Retreat Experience

What to Expect During the Retreat

Congregational Worship

Uplifting worship in Telugu, English, and Hindi, leading all worshippers into a deep presence of God.

Biblical Preaching

Clear, practical, and faith-building exposition of God's Word tailored specifically for Christian young people.

Action Songs & Games

Joyous interaction, energetic action songs, and icebreaker activities to foster genuine Christian friendships.

Fellowship Meals

Complimentary morning tea, shared lunch, and evening refreshments lovingly prepared and served on site.

Guidelines for Attendees

To ensure a blessed and orderly spiritual environment, please observe these simple guidelines:

  • Bring your Bible & Note: Carry your printed Holy Bible (Telugu or English), a notebook, and a pen to write down key notes.
  • Punctuality: Arrive before 9:00 AM to participate fully in the opening fellowship and opening prayer.
  • Reverence & Decorum: Maintain modesty and respect within the church sanctuary, keeping mobile phones silenced during services.
  • Open Heart: Come prayerfully with an open heart to hear God's voice and experience spiritual renewal.

Venue & Travel Assistance

The retreat will be hosted at Wesley Church, Palliwada Village, Ramannapeta Mandal, Yadadri Bhuvanagiri District, Telangana (PIN: 508113).

Transport: Regular buses and shared autos are available from Ramannapeta and surrounding towns.
Help: Please contact church organizers for travel guidance.
