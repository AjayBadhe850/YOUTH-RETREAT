"Abide in Me, and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit of itself, unless it abides in the vine, neither can you, unless you abide in Me." — John 15:4

The One Day Youth Retreat 2026 is a sacred spiritual gathering organised under the Church of South India (CSI), Diocese of Karimnagar, Aler Group Church Council, Mothkur Pastorate at C.S.I. Wesley Church, Palliwada. Grounded in the divine motto 'CALLED. CHOSEN. LOVED. SENT.' and the theme 'WHY SHOULD WE NOT ABIDE IN CHRIST?' (John 15:4), this retreat empowers young believers through vibrant worship, biblical exposition, and fellowship.

Retreat Experience

What to Expect During the Retreat

Congregational Worship

Uplifting worship in Telugu, English, and Hindi, leading all worshippers into a deep presence of God.

Biblical Preaching

Clear, practical, and faith-building exposition of God's Word tailored specifically for Christian young people.

Action Songs & Games

Joyous interaction, energetic action songs, and icebreaker activities to foster genuine Christian friendships.

Fellowship Meals

Complimentary morning tea, shared lunch, and evening refreshments lovingly prepared and served on site.

Guidelines for Attendees

To ensure a blessed and orderly spiritual environment, please observe these simple guidelines:

  • Bring your Bible & Note: Carry your printed Holy Bible (Telugu or English), a notebook, and a pen to write down key notes.
  • Punctuality: Arrive promptly before 8:30 AM on 9 November 2026 (Monday) to participate fully in the opening fellowship and opening prayer.
  • Reverence & Decorum: Maintain modesty and respect within the church sanctuary, keeping mobile phones silenced during services.
  • Open Heart: Come prayerfully with an open heart to hear God's voice, abide in Christ, and experience spiritual renewal.

Venue & Travel Assistance

The retreat will be hosted at C.S.I. Wesley Church, Palliwada Village, Ramannapeta Mandal, Yadadri Bhuvanagiri District, Telangana (PIN: 508113).

Transport: Regular buses and shared autos are available from Ramannapeta and surrounding towns.
Help: Please contact church organizers for travel guidance.
