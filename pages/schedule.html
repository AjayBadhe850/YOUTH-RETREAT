Retreat Registration
Wesley Church Palliwada • Youth Retreat
100% Free Admission For All Youth
How to Participate:

Registration for the One-Day Youth Retreat is completely free! All youth from surrounding congregations, students, and young working professionals are warmly invited.

  • Spot Registration: Available at the entrance desk from 9:00 AM on retreat day.
  • Venue: Wesley Church Sanctuary, Palliwada Village, Ramannapeta Mandal (PIN 508113).
  • Complimentary: Retreat booklet, morning tea, fellowship lunch, and snacks provided on campus.
Need Help or Travel Directions?

Please contact church organizers or youth leaders for travel guidance to Palliwada.
