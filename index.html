Welcome to Wesley Church

WELCOME TO WESLEY CHURCH PALLIWADA

Wesley Church Palliwada warmly welcomes all youth, students, and believers in the precious name of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ. Nestled in Palliwada village in the Yadadri Bhuvanagiri district of Telangana, our church stands as a sanctuary of prayer, spiritual renewal, and active community fellowship for young people.

Vibrant Worship

Soulful Telugu, English, and Hindi congregational praise exalting our Lord and Savior.

Biblical Teaching

Practical Bible sessions guiding Christian youth through modern challenges with purity and conviction.

Youth Fellowship

Fun action songs, interactive discussions, and genuine bonding with youth from surrounding villages.

Fellowship Meals

Morning tea, shared lunch, and afternoon refreshments provided with love on the church grounds.
